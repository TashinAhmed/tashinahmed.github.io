Tashin Ahmed (তাশীন আহমেদ in Bangla/বাংলা & アハメド・タシン in Japanese/日本語) is a researcher & engineer specializing in systems-based AI, with expertise spanning Neural Network Architectures across diverse domains such as Computer Vision, Natural Language Understanding, Sequential Learning in AI. His focus lies in fundamental R&D, aiming to bridge data processing with terminal solutions while contributing to the development of True and Safe AGI.

Previously, he worked as an AI Research Engineer at Smart Studios Malta and as a Data Scientist at AriSaf Tech Japan K.K. He served as a Research Assistant in the Dept. of CSE, United International University, funded by the ICT Ministry of Bangladesh under the ICT Innovation Fund. Additionally, he contributed as a Research Volunteer at the Collaborative Robotics Lab - University of Virginia, and completed an internship at CSIR - Central Drug Research Institute, Uttar Pradesh, India.

His long-term research interests aim to address challenges in AI system development and play a vital role in advancing the AGI revolution.


Industrial Experiences: Classical Computer Vision, Orthophoto Analysis and Segmentation, Quantitative Analysis, Algorithmic Trading, High Frequency Trading, Optical Character Recognition (English, Bengali, Japanese), Medical Image Analysis, Board Game AI.

Long-term Research Interests: Neuro-Dynamic Programming, Cellular Automaton, Swarm (AI) Intelligence, Multimodal & Reincarnating RL, Meta Learning, Core Neural Network Architectures.
